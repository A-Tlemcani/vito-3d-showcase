For restaurant owners

Your dining room, in 3D, on your own website.

This page is a demo. Here is how we would make yours, with your real room, your real dishes and no invented photos.

  1. 1 · Scan visitA two-person team comes before service (about 90 minutes). We scan the dining room and terrace with a LiDAR phone and a gimbal camera to make a Gaussian-splat 3D scan: real light, real textures.
  2. 2 · Signature dishesFive to eight dishes shot on a turntable (photogrammetry) and turned into light 3D models, so guests see the plate before they book.
  3. 3 · The websiteScroll story like the one above, menu, live table booking connected to your system (TheFork, Zenchef or your own), Google-ready structured data.
  4. 4 · Launch & careFast on phones (3D loads after the page), works without 3D, updated each season when the menu changes.

What you provide: one morning of access and your menu. What you get: a site guests remember, and bookings without platform commission.
